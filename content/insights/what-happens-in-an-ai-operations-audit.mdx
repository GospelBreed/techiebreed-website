Before we build anything, we want to understand how your business really runs. The AI Operations Audit is how we do that. It's the starting point we recommend for most businesses, and here is what it involves.

## Step one: a conversation about your business

We start by talking to you and, where it helps, members of your team. We ask how work comes in, who handles it, which tools you use and where things tend to get stuck. There are no wrong answers — the messier it feels, the more useful the conversation.

## Step two: mapping how work moves

Next, we map the main processes in your business: how an enquiry becomes a customer, how a request gets completed, how reports are produced. Seeing the steps written down often makes the repetitive parts obvious.

## Step three: finding the bottlenecks

With the map in front of us, we look for the places where time is lost:

- tasks repeated by hand on a schedule
- information copied between tools
- steps that wait on one person
- follow-ups that depend on someone remembering

## Step four: clear recommendations

You get a short, plain-language set of recommendations, in priority order. For each one we explain the problem it solves, what we would build, and what it would take. You decide what happens next.

> AI recommends, people approve, and the system does the work. Your team stays in control throughout.

## What you need to prepare

Very little. A list of the tools you use and some time with the people who do the work day to day is enough to get started.
