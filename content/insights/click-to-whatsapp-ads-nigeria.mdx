You boost a post or run an ad with a "Send WhatsApp message" button. The chats arrive. Some days your phone does not stop buzzing. And yet at the end of the month, when you compare what you spent on Meta with what actually landed in your account, the numbers do not seem to talk to each other.

That gap is the most common complaint about WhatsApp ads in Nigeria. The ad usually is not the problem. The problem is what happens after the click: slow first replies, the same questions answered by hand fifty times a day, no follow-up for the people who went quiet, and no reliable way to tell which ad produced a paying customer.

This guide shows you how to run Click-to-WhatsApp ads properly, from the setup choices in Ads Manager to the first reply, the follow-up, and the measurement that tells you your real cost per sale. It is written for Nigerian business owners and marketers who want to learn to run these campaigns themselves, and it is honest about where you will need a technical hand.

## What a Click-to-WhatsApp ad is (and why it suits Nigerian buyers)

A Click-to-WhatsApp ad, often shortened to CTWA, is a Facebook or Instagram ad whose button opens a WhatsApp chat with your business instead of sending people to a website. The customer taps, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message, and they can start talking to you straight away.

For many Nigerian buyers, that is exactly how they want to shop. People want to ask a question, see another photo, confirm delivery to their area and negotiate a little before they pay. A chat handles all of that better than a product page. The reach of the channel is also hard to ignore: the Nigeria Smartphone Study 2025 by Orange Group and KPMG, [reported by Brand Spur](https://brandspurng.com/2026/09/26/whatsapp-installed-on-95-of-nigerian-smartphones-study-finds/), found WhatsApp installed on 95% of the smartphones it surveyed across 12 major cities.

The trade-off is that a chat is not a sale. A website checkout either completes or it doesn't, and your analytics records it. A WhatsApp conversation can drift for days, end with "I'll get back to you", or close with a bank transfer that nobody links back to the ad. Running CTWA ads well means managing both halves: the marketing and the operations behind it.

## Before you spend: get these four things ready

Most failed campaigns fail before the ad goes live. Check these first.

**1. A WhatsApp Business number connected to your Facebook Page.** Meta's own [guide to creating ads that click to WhatsApp](https://whatsappbusiness.com/products/create-ads-that-click-to-whatsapp/) starts in Ads Manager, and the WhatsApp number connected to your Page is the one the ad opens. Use the WhatsApp Business app or the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), not a personal account.

**2. Someone, or something, ready to reply within minutes.** Decide now who answers ad chats, during which hours, and what happens at night and on Sundays. If the honest answer is "whoever sees it first", fix that before you spend a naira. Our guide to [real estate lead follow-up](/insights/real-estate-lead-follow-up-automation-nigeria) explains why response speed matters so much, and the same logic applies to anything sold through chat.

**3. Answers to the questions every ad will trigger.** Write down your current prices, sizes or packages, delivery areas and fees, payment options and turnaround times. You will reuse this in your ad copy, your welcome message and any automated replies.

**4. A simple place to log every ad lead.** A shared sheet is enough to start: date, name, phone number, which ad they came from, what they asked about, the outcome and the amount paid. Without this, you will not be able to measure anything later.

## How to set up a Click-to-WhatsApp ad in Ads Manager

You can create a simple ad from the Advertise option in the WhatsApp Business app's business tools, and for a first test that is fine. Ads Manager gives you far more control over objectives, audiences, budgets and reporting, so that is the route described here. Meta changes its menus from time to time, so treat this as the logic rather than a click-by-click script. If you have never used Ads Manager, start with our step-by-step guide to [running Facebook ads in Nigeria](/insights/how-to-run-facebook-ads-nigeria), which covers the ad account, paying in naira and ARCON approval.

### Step 1: Choose an objective that matches what you actually want

Meta lets you send people to WhatsApp under several objectives, including Engagement, Traffic, Leads and Sales. The objective tells Meta's delivery system which people to look for. Optimise for conversations and Meta finds people likely to start a chat, which is not always the same as people likely to buy.

Meta's own published case studies show why this matters. **VIP Leather Craft**, an Indonesian leather goods retailer using the WhatsApp Business app, tested ads optimised for purchases against its usual ads optimised for conversations. According to [Meta's success story](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/vip-leather-craft/), the purchase-optimised campaign produced 28% more sales at a 21% lower cost per purchase. **Nutryfit**, an Indian nutrition platform, tested lead optimisation instead of conversation optimisation and, [according to Meta](https://whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/nutryfit/), saw a 12% lower cost per lead in a Meta A/B test run in April 2025.

These are vendor-published results from other markets, so do not read them as a promise of what you will get. The lesson that does transfer is this: **once Meta can see which chats became leads or sales, optimise for that, not for chats.** Until it can, starting with conversations is reasonable, as long as you track outcomes yourself.

### Step 2: Set WhatsApp as the conversion location

At the ad set level, in the conversion section, choose message destinations (or WhatsApp directly, depending on the objective). If you don't want chats split across Messenger and Instagram Direct, make sure only WhatsApp is selected. Check that the right Facebook Page, and therefore the right WhatsApp number, is chosen. This single setting is what turns an ordinary ad into a Click-to-WhatsApp ad.

### Step 3: Target only where you can deliver

If you only deliver in Lagos, do not pay to reach Kano. Set locations to the places you can actually serve, and be realistic about age and language. Broad interest targeting often works well on Meta now, but location is a hard business constraint, not a preference.

### Step 4: Write the creative to pre-qualify, not just to attract

The cheapest chats are not always the best ones. An ad that shows the price, or at least a "from" price, and says where you deliver will produce fewer but better conversations than a vague "DM for price". If people are going to vanish once they hear the price, it is cheaper for that to happen before they click. The same applies to your organic posts; our guide to [automating Instagram DMs](/insights/automate-instagram-dms-nigeria) explains why "DM for price" also carries consumer-law risk in Nigeria.

This is also where AI earns its place in your marketing. Use an AI assistant to draft five or six versions of the headline and copy for each audience, then pick two to test. Ask it to rewrite the same offer for a busy professional, a student and a parent. You still decide what is true and on-brand; the AI simply saves you the blank page.

### Step 5: Write the pre-filled message carefully

In the ad's message template section, you set the text that appears in the customer's WhatsApp box when they tap. Make it specific to the ad, for example: "Hi, I saw your ad for the 3-in-1 blender. Do you deliver to Ikeja?" A specific opening tells your team, or your automation, exactly which product and ad the person came from, and gives the customer an easy first step.

### Step 6: Set a test budget and a test period

Run a modest budget for long enough to see a pattern. Several of Meta's published tests ran for around two weeks. Change one thing at a time, such as the creative or the objective, so you know what made the difference.

## The first five minutes: where most of your ad spend is won or lost

When someone taps your ad, their interest is at its peak. What they see next decides whether you paid for a customer or for a notification.

**Reply instantly, even if a person cannot.** At minimum, set up a greeting that confirms you've received the message, answers the most likely question (usually price or availability) and asks one qualifying question. In the WhatsApp Business app, greeting messages and quick replies cover the basics. Beyond that, you need an assistant connected to your product information. Our guide to [setting up a WhatsApp AI assistant](/insights/whatsapp-ai-assistant-for-business-nigeria) covers what it should and should not handle, and how it hands over to a person.

**Ask questions that sort buyers from browsers.** Two or three questions are usually enough: what they want, where they are, and when they need it. A customer who answers all three is a lead. One who asks "How much?" and disappears is not, and should not count as one in your reporting.

**Make paying easy.** If someone is ready to buy, send clear payment details or a payment link straight away. Every extra message between "I'll take it" and payment is a chance for them to change their mind.

**Hand over cleanly.** Negotiation, custom orders, complaints and anything your written answers do not cover should go to a person, with the conversation history attached, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.

## Follow up inside the free window

Many people who chat do not buy on the first day. A well-timed follow-up, such as a reminder that the item is still in stock, the answer to a question they asked, or a delivery date, often closes the sale. For businesses on the WhatsApp Business Platform, Meta's pricing rewards this.

According to Meta's [WhatsApp Business Platform pricing documentation](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/) (updated 30 September 2026):

- When someone messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on the Android or iOS app and you reply within the 24-hour customer service window, your reply opens a **free entry point window that may stay open for up to 7 days**. While it is open, Meta does not charge for marketing, utility, authentication or service messages.
- Replies generated by **Meta Business Agent**, Meta's own AI agent, are still charged inside that window, on a per-token basis.
- Outside free windows, Meta charges per delivered message, by message category and by the recipient's country. Nigeria has its own rate on the rate card. Since 1 October 2026, service replies are also charged once a business phone number has used its 1,000 free service messages for the month.

The practical lesson: **reply fast, because a fast reply unlocks the free window, then use that window to follow up.** Plan a short sequence, such as a same-day answer, a next-day reminder and a final nudge a few days later, and stop when the customer says no. If you use the free WhatsApp Business app and reply by hand, these Platform charges don't apply in the same way, but the follow-up discipline matters just as much.

Remember that marketing messages still need consent. WhatsApp's [Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) requires opt-in before you message people and clear ways to opt out, and the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 gives people the right to object to direct marketing. Someone asking about one product has not agreed to your weekly promo broadcast.

## How to measure what your WhatsApp ads actually earn

Ads Manager will happily show you your cost per messaging conversation. That number is useful, but it is not the one that pays your rent. What you want is cost per qualified lead and cost per sale, by ad.

### The manual method (start here)

Use the sheet you set up earlier. For every ad chat, record the ad it came from (your specific pre-filled message makes this easy), whether it became a qualified lead, and whether and how much they paid. At the end of each week, divide each ad's spend by its qualified leads and by its sales. Within two or three weeks you will usually see that one ad brings cheap chats and another brings buyers, which is the decision you actually needed to make. To compare WhatsApp ads with your other channels on the same basis, see our guide to [measuring marketing ROI for a small business](/insights/measure-marketing-roi-small-business-nigeria).

AI helps here too. Paste a week of anonymised chat summaries into an AI assistant and ask it to group the most common objections and questions. Those become your next ad's copy and your next set of quick replies.

### The connected method (when volume justifies it)

On the WhatsApp Business Platform, the first message from an ad click carries a click identifier (`ctwa_clid`) in the data your system receives. Meta's [Conversions API for Business Messaging](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/business-messaging) lets your systems send events such as a submitted lead or a purchase back to Meta using that identifier. Two things follow: Ads Manager can report real leads and sales against each ad, and Meta can optimise delivery towards people who behave like your buyers, which is what lead- and purchase-optimised campaigns rely on.

This is not an afternoon job. It needs a Platform integration, the right permissions, and a reliable way to mark a chat as a lead or a sale, usually in your CRM or order system. Events also need to be sent soon after they happen, so the reporting has to be automatic rather than something someone remembers to do on Friday. It is exactly the kind of connection where [business systems integration](/what-we-do#integrations) pays for itself.

## An illustrative example

*This is an illustrative walkthrough, not a client case study or a real result.*

A small home-appliance seller in Lagos runs two Click-to-WhatsApp ads for the same blender. Ad A says "DM for price". Ad B shows the price, says "Free delivery within Lagos Mainland" and pre-fills "Hi, I want the blender. Do you deliver to [area]?"

Every chat gets an instant greeting with the price, delivery fee and payment details, plus one question: "Which area should we deliver to?" Anyone who answers is logged as a qualified lead. People who asked but did not pay get a reminder the next day, inside the free window.

After two weeks the owner reads the sheet, not just Ads Manager. Ad A had the lower cost per chat. Ad B had fewer chats but a lower cost per sale. Budget moves to Ad B, and the next test is a new creative in Ad B's style. None of this needed advanced technology. It needed a written process and a sheet that someone actually kept up to date.

## Common mistakes to avoid

**Judging ads by cost per conversation.** Cheap chats from people who were never going to buy are expensive.

**Hiding the price.** "DM for price" generates conversations, not customers, and it trains your team to type the same number all day.

**No plan for out-of-hours chats.** Ads deliver at 11pm. If nobody answers until 9am, you paid for interest you never used.

**One WhatsApp number on one person's phone.** When that person is busy, sick or on leave, the ad spend keeps running and nothing happens.

**Broadcasting to everyone who ever chatted.** Without opt-in, this risks reports and restrictions on your number, and it is not what those people agreed to.

**Changing everything at once.** If you change the creative, audience and objective in the same week, you will not know what worked.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much should I spend on Click-to-WhatsApp ads in Nigeria?

There is no correct number, and anyone quoting one without knowing your margins is guessing. Start with a test budget you can afford to learn with, run it for about two weeks, and work out your cost per sale. Scale only the ads where that number still leaves you a profit.

### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API to run these ads?

No. Ads can open a chat in the free WhatsApp Business app. You need the WhatsApp Business Platform (API) when you want several staff on one number, automation connected to your systems, or conversion data sent back to Meta through the Conversions API.

### Is it free to message people who come from my WhatsApp ad?

On the WhatsApp Business Platform, if the person messages you from the ad on the mobile app and you reply within 24 hours, Meta's pricing documentation says a free entry point window opens for up to 7 days, during which marketing, utility, authentication and service messages are not charged. Meta Business Agent replies are still charged. Meta can update pricing at the start of each quarter, so check its current pricing page before you plan.

### Why do people click my ad and then not reply?

Usually because the pre-filled message did not match what they wanted to ask, the first reply was slow or generic, or the price was a surprise. Make the opening message specific, reply instantly with the information they came for, and ask one easy question.

### Can AI reply to my WhatsApp ad leads?

Yes, if it answers only from your approved information and hands over to a person for anything it cannot handle. WhatsApp's policy requires a clear path to a human. If you use Meta's own Business Agent, budget for its per-token charges.

### Which ad gets credit when someone pays by bank transfer days later?

Only the one you record. That is why the lead sheet, or a CRM that links each chat to its ad, matters. With a Platform setup, you can also send the purchase back to Meta using the ad's click identifier, so Ads Manager reports it too.
