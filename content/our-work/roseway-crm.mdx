## The problem before TechieBreed

Roseway Resident Services does two jobs at once: winning new property-management partners, and caring for the residents who live in those partners' communities. Sales conversations, partner details, resident information and resident requests lived in different places, which made it hard to see what was due today — and easy for an at-risk resident or a follow-up to slip through the cracks.

## What the business was doing manually

- Keeping prospective partners and their communities in separate lists and spreadsheets
- Tracking calls, emails, LinkedIn messages and meetings by memory and inbox
- Working out pipeline value and win rate by hand
- Logging resident requests and chasing their status across maintenance, billing and lease questions
- Pulling numbers together for reports

## What we built

We built the **Roseway CRM** — one place for the whole journey, from the first conversation with a property partner to everyday resident care.

**Dashboard.** Every day starts with open leads, hot prospects, consultations and pipeline revenue up top, deals followed through the leasing and onboarding stages, and the most recent opportunities ready to pick up.

**Leads.** Every prospective partner — the contact, the community and its management company, size, asset class and resident-experience score — filterable by stage, temperature or source, with import and export in seconds.

**Pipeline.** Each deal moves across a board from new lead to contacted, qualified, discovery call, proposal and pilot term. Drag a card forward and total pipeline value, average deal size and win rate update live.

**Outreach.** Every touch across email, calls, LinkedIn and meetings is logged, so the team sees at a glance who replied and what's booked next.

**Properties and residents.** Once a partner is won, its communities appear with their units, residents and open caseload. The residents directory shows everyone across partner communities with their status, experience score and open cases.

## What changed

Resident requests now flow through a **case pipeline**: every case is tagged by priority and category — from maintenance to billing and lease questions — and moves from intake to in progress to awaiting resident, with overdue items flagged in red. A single **tasks** list pulls every follow-up together, pipeline calls and case deadlines alike, so the team always knows exactly what's due today.

**Reports and analytics** show the health of the whole operation — open cases, average resolution time, at-risk residents and how the caseload breaks down by priority — all updating live. Growing partnerships and caring for residents now happen in one system instead of many.
