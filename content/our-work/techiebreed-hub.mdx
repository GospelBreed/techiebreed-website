## The problem before TechieBreed

Running a cohort-based course means juggling many moving parts: start dates, pre-recorded lessons, weekly assignments, live classes, recordings, student questions, community announcements and certificates. Spread across separate tools, students lose track of where to go next and the team spends its time answering the same questions.

## What the business was doing manually

- Sending students class links, dates and reminders
- Sharing lessons and the course outline in different places
- Posting recordings after each live session and answering "where's the replay?"
- Handling questions about classes, replays and assignments one by one
- Tracking who finished the course and issuing certificates

## What we built

We built the **TechieBreed Academy Hub** — live classes, lessons, community and certificates, all in one place.

**Mission Control.** The home page counts down to the cohort start date, walks each student through a five-minute setup checklist and shows exactly where to jump back in — from the next lesson to meeting the cohort.

**My Course.** The whole AI-Powered Digital Marketing programme: four weeks, 33 lessons and a practical assignment every week — from market research and strategy to content, paid ads and automation, through to analytics and a final capstone. Each lesson opens with a pre-recorded video with the week's outline beside it; students learn at their own pace, mark it done and continue.

**Live sessions.** Every weekend there's a live class with the facilitator. Before class day, students can preview the live room to check their camera and mic, and drop questions in the room chat.

**Community.** A news feed keeps students close to the team and their cohort with announcements, tips and introductions, and Messages lets them talk directly with support or chat with their class.

## What changed

Missed a class? Recordings appear in **Replays**, usually within 24 hours. VIP students keep every replay for 12 months and unlock the **VIP Vault** of templates, prompt packs and guides.

With **Earn Cash**, every student gets a referral link and earns 20% on every enrolment that comes through it, paid straight to their bank account. When a student finishes all 33 lessons and submits their four assignments, their **certificate unlocks** automatically, ready to download and share. And if they're ever stuck, **Techie**, the built-in help assistant, and the help page answer common questions about classes, replays and assignments.

Everything a student needs — learn, join live, connect, catch up, earn and get certified — now lives in one hub.
